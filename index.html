LQR gain is recalculated from the linearised model when you press Apply design. Swing-up uses energy shaping and the nonlinear equations to calculate the requested cart force; motor voltage/current, force slew and track limits still apply. At exact hanging with zero motion, the controller has no direction to start pumping, so a transparent, user-configurable startup excitation breaks the symmetry and is still limited by the motor model.

Changing a parameter does not change the running model until you apply the design. Applying the design restarts the run for a clean comparison.

Engineering limitations: this is a transparent lumped-parameter simulation, not a validated digital twin. It assumes a rigid uniform rod, a solid spherical bob, viscous friction, a quasi-static DC motor model, ideal bidirectional voltage drive, and no backlash, belt elasticity, wheel slip, driver dead-zone, current-loop dynamics, sensor noise, structural flex, or MCU/communication delay beyond sample-and-hold. Track end-stops are simplified. Validate equations and parameters against the actual rig before fabrication or safety decisions.
